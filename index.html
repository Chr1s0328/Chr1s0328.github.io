01 — About

Research at the intersection of vision and worlds.

I work toward structured, editable, and physically meaningful representations of our visual world.

I am a third-year undergraduate student majoring in Software Engineering and currently work as a research intern.

My recent work spans feedforward 3D editing, simulation-ready 4D reconstruction, and engine-native scene reconstruction with editable objects and lighting.

I enjoy turning visual observations into representations that can be understood, manipulated, and used by downstream systems.
